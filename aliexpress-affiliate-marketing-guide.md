# AliExpress Affiliate Marketing: A Beginner’s Guide to Product Promotion and Commissions

AliExpress affiliate marketing can be an option for bloggers, content creators, and website owners who want to monetize useful product-related content. Instead of selling products directly, affiliates recommend relevant items and may earn commissions when readers complete qualifying purchases through tracked links.

However, affiliate marketing is not simply about adding product links to a website. It requires audience research, helpful content, appropriate product selection, and a clear understanding of the affiliate program’s rules.

In this guide, you will learn how AliExpress affiliate marketing works, how to create tracking links through Admitad, and how to build a practical content strategy for your website or blog.

What Is AliExpress?

AliExpress is an online marketplace where shoppers can browse products across categories such as electronics, clothing, home accessories, beauty products, and everyday essentials.

For affiliate marketers, the marketplace provides a wide range of product categories that may suit different audiences. For example, a technology blog might cover accessories and gadgets, while a home-focused website could publish buying guides about storage products or household tools.

The key is to select products that match your readers’ interests rather than promoting unrelated items.

What Is the AliExpress Affiliate Program?

The AliExpress Affiliate Program allows eligible publishers to promote participating products and potentially earn commissions from qualifying transactions.

Affiliate marketers generally use special tracking links to connect a visitor’s click with a potential purchase. Commission eligibility depends on the program’s current conditions, including product eligibility, attribution rules, and other restrictions.

Publishers using Admitad can check the available AliExpress offer and use the network’s link-generation tools when their account and the offer permit it.

Important: Commission rates, payment terms, and eligible products can change. Always review the current offer details in your affiliate account instead of relying on outdated figures or assuming that every purchase will generate a commission.

How Does AliExpress Affiliate Marketing Work?

The process usually involves five steps:

Join an eligible affiliate offer: Review the available AliExpress offer and confirm its requirements.

Generate a tracking link: Use the affiliate network’s approved tool to create a tracked URL.

Publish useful content: Recommend relevant products through reviews, comparisons, tutorials, and buying guides.

Attract targeted visitors: Reach readers who are actively researching a product or trying to solve a specific problem.

Monitor results: Review clicks, qualifying transactions, and approved commissions using the reporting tools available to you.

A click does not guarantee a sale, and a sale does not always qualify for commission. Returns, cancellations, attribution rules, and offer restrictions can affect the final result.

Seven Tips for Promoting AliExpress Products

1. Choose Products Relevant to Your Audience

Product relevance is an important part of affiliate content. Start by understanding your readers’ interests, needs, and budgets.

For example, a website about technology could publish guides to useful accessories, while a lifestyle blog might cover practical home products.

Before recommending an item, check its specifications, seller information, available options, and other details that matter to buyers. Avoid making unsupported claims about quality or performance.

2. Create Helpful Product Reviews

Product reviews can answer questions that shoppers have before purchasing.

A useful review should explain:

What the product is designed to do

Its main features and specifications

Who may find it useful

What buyers should check before ordering

Potential limitations or compatibility considerations

If you have personally tested a product, describe your experience accurately. If you have not tested it, do not imply that you have. Clearly distinguish verified information from seller descriptions and your own analysis.

3. Publish Product Comparisons

Comparison articles help readers evaluate alternatives using consistent criteria.

You could compare products based on features, compatibility, materials, intended use, specifications, or other relevant factors. Explain which type of buyer might prefer each option.

Avoid declaring a product the best without evidence. A balanced comparison can be more useful than a promotional article that ignores disadvantages.

4. Use SEO to Attract Relevant Traffic

Search engine optimization can help your content reach people looking for product information.

Start with keyword research and identify the questions your target audience is asking. Use relevant phrases naturally in your title, headings, introduction, and body text.

Possible article formats include:

Product review guides

Product A versus Product B comparisons

Buying guides for a specific audience

Tutorials explaining how to use a product

Lists of products for a particular task

Prioritize search intent and helpful information instead of repeating a keyword excessively. No keyword strategy can guarantee a particular search ranking.

5. Add Clear Calls to Action

Readers should understand what to do if they want to explore a product.

Useful calls to action include:

View product details

Check the current listing

Compare available options

Explore product specifications

Place links where they naturally support the information. Make sure the link text accurately describes the destination, and avoid adding so many links that the article becomes difficult to read.

6. Disclose Affiliate Links Clearly

Readers should know when you may earn a commission from their purchases. Add a clear disclosure near the beginning of the article and before or near affiliate links.

For example:

Affiliate Disclosure: This article contains affiliate links. If you make a qualifying purchase through these links, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

Adapt the wording to your actual affiliate relationships and the rules that apply to your audience and location.

7. Track Performance and Improve Your Content

Use the reports available from your affiliate network to understand how your content performs.

Depending on the available reporting features, useful metrics may include clicks, conversion rates, approved transactions, and commissions.

If an article receives visitors but few affiliate clicks, consider whether the links are relevant and easy to find. If readers click but do not convert, review the audience’s intent, the product selection, and the information provided before the click.

Make changes based on meaningful data rather than assuming that every low-performing article needs more affiliate links.

Common AliExpress Affiliate Marketing Mistakes

Beginners should avoid these common problems:

Using ordinary links instead of tracking links: Standard marketplace URLs do not automatically provide affiliate attribution.

Promoting irrelevant products: Recommendations that do not match the audience’s needs can reduce trust.

Making unsupported promises: Avoid guaranteeing product quality, delivery times, savings, or affiliate income.

Ignoring program rules: Check restrictions on traffic sources, paid promotion, and permitted destinations.

Expecting immediate income: Results depend on traffic quality, reader intent, conversion behavior, and the offer’s conditions. Earnings are not guaranteed.

Neglecting content updates: Product details, availability, prices, and affiliate terms can change. Review important pages periodically and update outdated information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Costs depend on your chosen publishing method. You may be able to publish content on an existing website without paying for additional hosting, while building a new website or using paid marketing may involve expenses. Check the affiliate offer’s current joining requirements.

Can beginners earn money with AliExpress affiliate marketing?

Beginners may earn commissions if they meet the program requirements and generate qualifying purchases. However, there is no guaranteed income. Results depend on audience relevance, content quality, traffic, conversion rates, and current commission rules.

Can I use AliExpress links in blog posts?

Yes, when your promotional method follows the affiliate program’s rules. Use generated tracking links for affiliate promotion and disclose the relationship clearly.

Can I promote AliExpress products on GitHub?

GitHub is primarily a platform for software development and project collaboration. You can publish relevant educational Markdown documentation in a suitable public repository, but avoid turning repositories into spam or using GitHub in ways that violate its current policies. Check both GitHub’s rules and the affiliate offer’s permitted traffic sources before adding promotional links.

How often should I update affiliate articles?

Review articles periodically, especially when product details, prices, availability, or affiliate terms may have changed. Update claims only when you can verify the information.
